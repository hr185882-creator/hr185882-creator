# Authorship and AI-Assistance Disclosure

## Human responsibility

Hasan Raza Kazmi directs the research questions, analytical framework, evidence standards, product architecture, editorial judgment, quality assurance, and publication decisions for the projects represented in this portfolio.

## AI assistance

AI systems may assist with:

- code generation and debugging;
- document organization and formatting;
- synthesis of user-supplied or cited material;
- interface prototyping;
- test-case generation;
- drafting language for review;
- repetitive data transformation.

AI assistance does not replace source verification, legal or ethical review, analytical accountability, or final human judgment.

## Source rule

AI-generated output is not treated as evidence. Claims must be supported by identifiable records, official material, original reporting, or clearly labeled analytical inference.

## Publication rule

Before publication, substantive claims should be reviewed for:

- evidentiary support;
- source independence;
- uncertainty and counterarguments;
- privacy and identification risks;
- defamation and reputational harm;
- appropriate distinction between association, allegation, and culpability;
- date and version accuracy.

## Corrections

Material corrections should be documented in the relevant repository or product changelog. Where a public claim changes, the revision should preserve the earlier state, explain what changed, and identify the evidence that caused the revision.
